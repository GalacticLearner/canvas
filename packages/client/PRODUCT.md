# Canvas
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Electron, React, TypeScript, Canvas 2D; fixed by plan.md. Desktop app screens use a code-first build.

## Users
Students and instructors collaborating in labs and classrooms, often on mid-range Windows laptops and bright projectors.

## Product Purpose
A standalone shared whiteboard with LAN-only TCP collaboration and complete offline local editing.

## Operating Context
Long drawing sessions, a local server, room-code admission, multiple simultaneous clients, touch and stylus input. Internet is unavailable at runtime.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Drawing, diagrams, mind maps, sticky notes, images, text, frames, presentation, chat, role controls, recovery, versions and exports. See plan.md for normative semantics and acceptance criteria. No claims of achieved performance until measured.

## Brand Commitments
Canvas is the hero. Calm, precise workshop tone. No purple-blue gradients, glass decoration, emoji icons or generic SaaS layout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Light/dark/high contrast; WCAG AA; keyboard access; reduced motion; UI scales 100/125/150 percent; 1100×650 through 4K.

## Assumptions
The plan explicitly delegates ambiguous decisions and prohibits follow-up questions. Product facts above come from its brief; no interview was needed or substituted with invented answers. Public Sans for controls and Newsreader for the connect heading are implementation choices, not user-specified facts.
